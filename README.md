# Data Matters Workshop: AI and Deep Learning

## Overview

**Summary.** Artificial intelligence has accelerated at an extraordinary pace over the past decade, reshaping how we analyze data, build software, and solve complex problems. From large‑scale language models to autonomous systems, many of today’s breakthroughs—including emerging agentic AI workflows—are powered by advances in machine learning, especially deep learning. These methods now drive progress in natural language processing, computer vision, speech translation, biomedical imaging, robotics, and countless other domains. This course offers a clear, accessible overview of AI with a special focus on deep learning’s role in modern systems. You’ll explore foundational definitions, the major types of AI, and the historical developments that led to the resurgence of neural networks. We’ll examine how improvements in data availability and computing infrastructure enabled today’s deep learning revolution, and how these models now serve as the backbone of agentic AI pipelines that can plan, reason, and act across tasks.

**Why This Course.** This course is designed for anyone who wants a clearer understanding of AI and deep learning—what they are, where they’re used, and the strengths and limitations of these approaches. You’ll explore how modern AI systems apply deep learning across different applications, as well as the trade‑offs and challenges that come with these methods. The focus is on building conceptual understanding rather than teaching specific frameworks or guiding you through model development.

**What Will Participants Learn.** The course will focus on the following topics:
- What is AI, machine learning and deep learning?
- History of AI and machine learning
- Types of AI
- Types of machine learning tasks
- Types of deep learning architectures
- Applications of deep learning and neural networks in image processing, natural language processing, robotics, computer vision, biomedical, and health care.
